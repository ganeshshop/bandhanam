❓ Common questions

Is my data safe?
All data stays in your phone's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Backup/export anytime via the 💾 button top-right.

Does it work offline?
Yes. After first load, the app caches itself. You can build invitations and manage guests even without internet — sharing via WhatsApp needs connection.

Can guests RSVP without the app?
Yes. They just tap the WhatsApp link you send. The invitation opens in their phone browser — no install required.

Can I get a refund?
Yes — full refund within 7 days if you haven't used the paid features. Just email your UPI transaction ID.
